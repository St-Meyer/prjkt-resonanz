## About

PRJKT Resonanz is a solo-developed indie game.
A personal project built to tell a story.

You play a woman called **Lyra**, with a subtle magical gift in a society that has forgotten to feel. She can perceive **Resonances**: shimmering energy portals that open near deeply traumatized people, gateways to their inner worlds. Inside, she fights against abstract manifestations of their pain.

But there's a rule Lyra learns to late and with an high payment.
